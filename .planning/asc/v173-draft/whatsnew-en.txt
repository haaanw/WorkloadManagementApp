Tuwa 1.7.3 turns your program into your daily guide.

Bring your training program once — text, PDF, photo, or just say it — and Tuwa runs it day by day. Each morning it proposes today's session with the volumes already adjusted to your recovery, and a quick check-in tunes the numbers before you start. One tap on Start today shows what your body looks like, what the app therefore suggests, and your adjusted numbers — for court, run, and conditioning days that means a duration and an effort ceiling on your own plan, never a new one.

New guided session mode: when you start a planned workout, the app becomes a focused space showing the current move and its target numbers. Log a set in one tap, and Tuwa advances to the next move with what's coming up always in view — supersets alternate automatically. Your session lives on the lock screen too, so a glance shows where you are without unlocking.

Also new: Apple Watch workouts arrive on their own, with your effort rating carried over; Trends now reads as a fatigue story, with plain-language readings and a breakdown page for every index; home-screen widgets for today's readiness and training load; a four-tab layout; pull to refresh; and the app opens instantly — your data syncs in the background instead of making you wait.
