Tuwa 1.7.2 makes describing the session the fastest way to log it.

Say what you did — "back squat, three sets of five at 130, last one felt heavy" — or type the same sentence, or use the keyboard's dictation mic. Tuwa turns it into an editable draft with every set filled in and ticked off. Nothing saves until you confirm it, and any number is still yours to correct by hand. Mid-session, the same thing works one set at a time. Manual entry is unchanged and still the way to fix anything. Voice logging is free on every tier.

Also in this release: the weight scale now shows its full range before you touch it, with your last set and today's target marked on the rule. Deleted workouts stay deleted across devices, profile settings sync completely, and charts label their values correctly in every language.
