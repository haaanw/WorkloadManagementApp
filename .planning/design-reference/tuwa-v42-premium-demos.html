v4.2 — premium motion + instrument forms

Two fixes demoed side by side. §1: the smoothness gap — the current system's fixed curves and dip-crossfade vs a spring-settle system with no dead frames. §2: the Profile problem — the raw rows and the stock iOS menu vs a full instrument form system. Click and press everything; A is always "current", B the proposal.

1 · Motion: curves → smooth springs

Why it reads "subtle but not premium": (a) tab content dips to invisible before fading back — a blink; (b) every animation is a fixed-duration curve — mechanical, stops dead; (c) press release snaps back at the same speed it went down. The proposal: press fast + settle slow, springs that glide into place, and NOTHING ever passes through zero opacity.

In SwiftUI terms: Motion tokens move from `timingCurve` to non-bouncy springs (`.spring(duration:bounce:0)` / `.smooth` family — interruptible + velocity-preserving by construction), tabCrossfade is rebuilt as a ZStack hand-off with no zero-dip, press styles get asymmetric down/up timing, and entrances get the rise+stagger treatment. Durations stay in the same premium-fast band (droll ≤300ms).
