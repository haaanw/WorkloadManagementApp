Tuwa design language v4 — the instrument direction

Three syntheses of Braun · Bang & Olufsen · 1990s Contax on real Tuwa surfaces (Home hero, metric rows, the verdict card, tab bar). Common to all three: the serif and halftone are retired; numerals become the voice (mono/tabular "dial" digits); one tiny functional accent used like a power dot or index mark; scales get real ticks; controls look like controls. Motion (applied later, per Emil Kowalski's framework): crisp, sub-300ms, strong ease-out, mechanical detents with haptics.

T1 · Functional — Braun (Rams / ET66)

Warm matte cream-grey body, round dial motif for readiness, convex pill keys, ONE Braun-orange dot. The friendliest of the three; closest to a beloved household object.
