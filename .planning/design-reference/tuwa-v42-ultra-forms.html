What makes it ultra (the Relief Law, DESIGN.md v4.2)

1 · Two reliefs, one law. RAISED = outer hairline + 1px top-highlight inside (milled edge). DEBOSSED = darker gradient + inner top shade + 1px bottom-highlight (a pocket cut into the metal). Every surface is one or the other — flat dies.

2 · Values live in readout wells. Every reading — form values, stepper counts, picker selections — sits in a debossed mono well with a micro-caps unit. The well is fixed-width; digits change, metal doesn't.

3 · Press inverts relief. Raised keys become recessed under the finger (highlight dies, inner shadow appears, 0.5px drop) in ~85ms; release springs back over ~300ms. That asymmetry + inversion IS the premium feel.

4 · Options are machined cells in a channel. The row opens a debossed channel holding butted raised cells with drilled selection dots — reads like a component bay, not a menu.

5 · Machined toggle. Knurled knob (3 engraved lines) sliding in a debossed channel; the channel turns ink when on and an engraved index tick appears. Spring glide, one haptic.

6 · Everything else inherits. Focus = recessed well + ink border. Destructive rows stay quiet zone-red text. Chevrons are drawn 1.5px ticks that rotate on the spring.

SwiftUI: all strokes + LinearGradients + overlay lines — zero .shadow(), law intact. The relief becomes two ViewModifiers (.raised / .debossed) in the form component file, so the whole app machines itself from two primitives.
