<objective>
Bundle Noto Sans SC (Regular + Medium), register them, assert their availability at launch, and rewrite FontTokens so every typography token resolves via a cascade descriptor — General Sans for Latin glyphs, Noto Sans SC for CJK glyphs.

Purpose: D-17, D-18, D-19. Without the cascade, every Chinese string post-P2 falls back to system PingFang SC and breaks the visual contract with General Sans. The cascade is a single-point change in FontTokens.swift that propagates to every Font.Tokens.* call site in the app.

Output: zh-Hans strings render with mixed-script harmony without per-string font logic; bundle size delta verified at most 8 MB.
</objective>
