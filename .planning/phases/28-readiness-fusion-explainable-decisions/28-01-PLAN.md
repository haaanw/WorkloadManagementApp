    - Sign constraint: positive-is-good signals (HRV z, sleep z) have positive locked weights; inverted signals (RHR z, temp z) are sign-corrected so a worse raw reading lowers Readiness. Increasing an HRV z monotonically increases Readiness; increasing RHR (worse) monotonically decreases it.
    - Zone mapping: Readiness below the low/moderate cut → .low; between → .moderate; above the moderate/high cut → .high (boundary-inclusive tests at each cut).

    - Ranked factors: returns factors ordered by absolute weighted contribution, each decomposable to (signal, raw value if available, z, weighted contribution).
    - Missing signal: a nil z is excluded and weights renormalize over present signals (carry a missingness flag; do NOT mean-impute — research §2.2 / codex §314).
