<objective>
Fix the same-day date-contract bug and introduce the generic experimental-arm storage that the rest of v1.6 plugs into — **without** adding any scoring model. This plan splits the conflated `CyclePredictionLog.date` into an explicit `predictionDate` (day the prediction is made) and `targetDate` (day it is about), makes Stage-2 resolution join actuals on `targetDate` only (closing the leak), enforces the prequential feature-cutoff at series construction, and replaces the hard-coded `*Baseline`/`*CycleAware` column pairs with a generic per-arm child store fed by an `ExperimentalArm` registry. The two existing predictors (`baseline`, `cycleAware`) are re-expressed as registered arms and must produce byte-identical numbers, preserving the Phase-20 cycle validation through the new unified path.

Pure-struct predictor stays pure (Foundation only); `ShadowAnalyticsService` stays the `@MainActor` SwiftData orchestrator; both new/changed models stay local-only and never-synced. Master activation flag stays OFF; no user-facing behavior changes.
</objective>
