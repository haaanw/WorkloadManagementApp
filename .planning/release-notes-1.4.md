## English (full)

Smarter recovery, faster logging, and now in Simplified Chinese.

• Cycle-aware recovery — If you track your cycle in Apple Health, Tuwa now factors it into your recovery baseline, so normal cycle-phase dips in HRV no longer read as declining recovery. Includes cycle context on your dashboard, fueling and recovery prompts, and gentle early flags for cycle irregularities.

• Now in Simplified Chinese (简体中文) — The entire app is fully localized.

• Faster workout logging — A new radial picker: press and drag to choose your sport and session type in a single gesture.

• Precise muscle targeting — Log against 33 specific muscle groups for a clearer view of where your training load actually lands.

• Stability and polish throughout.
