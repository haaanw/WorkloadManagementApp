# n=1 Dogfood Protocol

**Window:** 6 consecutive in-season weeks (must contain real scheduled matches).

**What n=1 validates:** verdict accuracy on the reference body, cross-modal carry direction (game→squat), match-proximity microdose trims, founder retention.
**What it cannot validate:** WTP (gate stays CLOSED — MID/LONG horizons stay frozen), non-founder retention, schedule generality. Sean-Ellis + card-on-file instrumentation stays built but dormant.

## Green-light criteria (all pre-registered)

1. **≥8 differing-verdict days** — days where verdict ≠ plan-as-written. Fewer ⇒ engine too timid to matter.
2. **≥60% of differing verdicts followed** — else founder doesn't trust his own product.
3. **≥70% of followed verdicts felt right** — judged next-day (session quality / soreness / match freshness), logged same-day, not retro-rated.
4. **≥2 match-proximity microdoses** that ended in fresh-legs matches.
5. **≥1 clearly-wrong verdict with an understood cause** — zero wrong verdicts in 6 weeks means insufficient scrutiny (kill-condition against motivated reasoning).

## Outcomes

- **Pass →** invest in recruiting the 5 real basketball validation users (screener: plays organized/regular ball, lifts ≥2×/wk in-season, self-manages, wearable or logs).
- **Fail →** tune engine on the diagnosed misses, repeat the window. No repositioning, no recruitment, no new horizons on a failed window.

## Accepted risks

- Commoditization clock (Garmin per-muscle readiness 2026) ticks during dogfood. Defense = basketball anchor + plan-awareness, which a wearable score can't copy.
- Founder bias — mitigated only by this pre-registration and criterion 5.
