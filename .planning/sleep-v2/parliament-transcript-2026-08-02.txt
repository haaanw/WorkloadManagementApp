=== CODEX OPENING ===
### Opening statement

I vote to replace the headroom-gain weighted mean with an explicit base-plus-quality adjustment:

`score = clamp(0…100, 0.80D + Σ[pᵢ × (Cᵢ − 80) / 20])`

Here `D` is the existing 0–100 duration-vs-need curve; therefore duration contributes exactly 0–80. Quality allocations are continuity **8**, regularity **6**, deep **3**, REM **3** points. Context profiles may redistribute those 20 points, but never change the 80/20 budgets; greater sleep pressure should affect `needTonight`, not duration’s weight.

These allocations are **JUDGMENT**, not fitted science. Continuity gets priority because Chinoy found sleep sensitivity ≥0.93 but wake specificity only 0.18–0.54 and inconsistent staging ([paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33378539/)); a newer comparison found stage κ **0.21–0.53** ([paper](https://academic.oup.com/sleepadvances/article/6/2/zpaf021/8090472)). Regularity remains substantial but below continuity: Windred found **20–48%** lower mortality across higher regularity quintiles, not improved next-day readiness ([paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37738616/)). Leeder’s athletes averaged **80.6%** efficiency versus **88.7%** in controls, supporting athlete-shifted continuity anchors ([paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22329779/)). Kill test: at ≥200 nights/20 athletes, any component with within-athlete partial correlation ≤0 with sleep-free next-day readiness loses its allocation; refit the remainder.

Keep H-11 unchanged: **q=1.00 → 85**, **q≥1.30 →100**, and existing sub-baseline anchors. These are **JUDGMENT**; if q≥1.30 occurs on <2% of stable-source nights, replace it with that source-athlete’s 90th percentile.

Tier C should max at exactly **90** with timing only, or **94** when continuity exists—both **JUDGMENT**. Do not renormalize missing evidence to 100: that would certify “excellent” from duration plus schedule alone. Kill this cap if, after ≥100 nights per tier, top-decile Tier-C sleep-free readiness trails Tier A by <2 points.

Strongest objection: tier caps punish device choice. True—but tier/confidence disclosure is preferable to false precision.

**Provisional vote: adopt two-part composition; confidence 0.82.**

=== CODEX REBUTTAL AND FINAL VOTE ===
### Cross-examination of Grok

Grok’s strongest point is arithmetic clarity: an explicit 80-point duration base plus a 20-point quality band makes the founder’s rule auditable and athlete-explainable. Rejecting the hidden 2.0 headroom gain is correct.

Its proposed formula, however, has a fatal defect:

`clamp((rawᵢ − 80) / 20, 0, 1)`

makes every quality value below 80 equivalent. Deep sleep at q=0.40→45 and q=0.85→75 would both contribute zero. That nullifies the athlete-shifted sub-baseline anchors and discards potentially useful signal. Use the signed adjustment from my opening instead; poor quality may reduce the duration-derived score, while excellent quality earns the final 20.

Grok also says “keep duration weight 0.50,” but its two-part arithmetic contains no 0.50 duration weight. Under the new composition, the meaningful constants are the fixed 80-point base and quality-point allocations. Existing context deltas therefore require translation into transfers within the 20-point quality budget; they cannot be applied unchanged.

I concede Grok’s stage re-anchor: revise q=1.00 from **85 to 80**. It gives every quality component the same semantic threshold—80 means “normal/good,” not failure—and q≥1.30→100 remains “excellent.” This is **JUDGMENT**; retain H-11’s <2%-reachability kill test.

I retain **continuity 8 / regularity 6 / deep 3 / REM 3**. Chinoy’s wake specificity of **0.18–0.54** supports caution even for continuity, while stage κ **0.21–0.53** argues against Grok increasing each stage allocation to 3.6. Windred’s **20–48% mortality association** cannot calibrate next-day readiness, but timing is measured more reliably than stages.

Tier C should not renormalize to 100: retain **90 timing-only / 94 with continuity**, explicitly as epistemic caps. Better kill test: across **200 paired nights/20 athletes**, mask Tier-A data to Tier C; lift the 90 cap to 95 only if masked predictions achieve **MAE ≤3** against full Tier-A scores.

**Final vote: adopt signed two-part 80+20 composition; confidence 0.87. Decisive reason: exact arithmetic without making sub-baseline quality evidence disappear.**
