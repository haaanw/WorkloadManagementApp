## Verification Notes

This copy intentionally avoids future-projection claims, plan-authoring claims, group-management claims, and any claim that cross-modal carry currently changes the verdict. Verified shipped surfaces include: planned-session verdicts, adjusted top-set numbers, one-line reasons, next match date, match-proximity microdose, match-tier logging, HealthKit HRV / RHR / sleep readiness, accept / keep / feel input, and next-day felt-right tracking.

## English Full Description

Tuwa helps amateur competitive basketball players who strength-train seriously stay in the strike zone every workout. You bring your own plan. Tuwa reads your recovery, recent load, and next match date, then gives a daily verdict for today's planned lift: go, modify, or hold.

Built for the athlete who has a league match, a squat day, and no support staff.

What Tuwa does:

- Gives a daily verdict on your planned strength session: go, modify, or hold.
- Shows an adjusted top-set number when today's plan needs a trim.
- Explains the verdict in one calm line, with confidence shown separately.
- Uses HealthKit HRV, resting heart rate, and sleep to build readiness context.
- Tracks training load, ACWR, and recent workout history.
- Lets you set your next match date so near-match sessions can become a microdose: capped top set, no back-offs.
- Logs basketball context as pickup, scrimmage, or match.
- Keeps your authored plan intact until you accept a suggestion.
- Lets you keep the plan, accept the adjustment, or log that you feel strong or rough.
- Asks the next day whether the change felt right, so your own judgment stays part of the loop.

Tuwa is not a generic recovery score, and it is not a replacement program. Whoop and Bevel can tell you how recovered you are. Program apps can give you their plan. TrainingPeaks can hold a plan without making today's decision. Tuwa sits in the middle: your plan, made safe and optimal for today.

Example: last night's game hammered your legs. Today's squat readiness is down, but your bench context stays separate. With a match close, Tuwa can turn the lift you already planned into a microdose instead of inventing a new workout.

Stay in your strike zone -- every workout.

## zh-Hans Full Description

Tuwa 帮助认真力量训练的业余竞技篮球爱好者，把每一次训练控制在今天合适的区间内。计划由你自己制定。Tuwa 读取恢复状态、近期负荷和下一场比赛日期，然后给今天的力量训练一个每日判断：照练、微调，或保持。

适合有联赛比赛、有深蹲日、但没有专业团队支持的自我管理型运动员。

Tuwa 可以做什么：

- 对今天计划中的力量训练给出每日判断：照练、微调，或保持。
- 当今天需要调整时，显示调整后的 top set 数字。
- 用一句平静、清楚的理由解释判断，并单独显示信心。
- 使用 HealthKit 中的 HRV、静息心率和睡眠来建立准备度背景。
- 追踪训练负荷、ACWR 和近期训练历史。
- 让你设置下一场比赛日期；比赛临近时，把你原本的训练缩成 microdose：封顶 top set，跳过 back-off。
- 记录篮球场景：野球、对抗赛，或正式比赛。
- 在你接受建议前，保留你原本写好的计划。
- 你可以接受调整、坚持原计划，或记录今天感觉更强/更累。
- 第二天询问这次调整是否“刚刚好”，让你的体感持续进入反馈。

Tuwa 不是泛泛的恢复分数，也不是替你写计划的应用。Whoop 和 Bevel 可以告诉你恢复得如何。写计划类应用会给你它们的计划。TrainingPeaks 可以保存计划，但不替你做今天的取舍。Tuwa 站在中间：你的计划，按今天的状态变得更安全、更合适。

例如：昨晚的比赛让腿很疲劳。今天深蹲准备度下降，但卧推背景保持独立。比赛临近时，Tuwa 可以把你已经计划好的训练缩成 microdose，而不是凭空生成一节新课。

每一次训练，都留在今天的 strike zone。
