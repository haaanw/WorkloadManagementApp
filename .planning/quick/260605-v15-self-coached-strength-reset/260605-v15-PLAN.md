<objective>
Re-calibrate Tuwa into the discussed v1.5 product-market fit direction:

Tuwa is a self-coaching system for serious amateur strength and hybrid athletes who train without a full-time coach. The app should help them plan future training, log workouts quickly, adapt live volume/content from recovery/load/history, and review strength progress over time.

Eliminate redundant visible product surfaces that dilute this: coach-first business workflow, cycle/hormone side quest, ACWR implementation settings, and broad "workload management for coaches" positioning.
</objective>
