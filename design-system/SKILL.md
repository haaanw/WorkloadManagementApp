Key facts: the language is "Field Notes" — warm stone planes, no shadows (relief instead), Instrument Sans UI voice, Fragment Mono annotation layer (≤12px, uppercase), Alpino display (marketing/slides only), travertine accent under the Accent Rule, five icon-derived metric hues, desaturated label-first zone colors, 12/8/pill corners, 8pt grid, light-only. Web motion reference: `ui_kits/website/motion.js` (rAF scroll-scrub scenes, reveals, count-ups — reduced-motion guarded).
