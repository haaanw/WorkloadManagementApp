# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Serious self-coached hybrid athletes who train sport skill, strength, and conditioning in parallel, often without direct access to coaching, physiotherapy, or sports-science support. The product also supports coaches and coach-athlete relationships, but the primary workflow is an athlete checking whether their authored plan should be executed, modified, or held today.

## Product Purpose

Tuwa is the sports-science staff layer for self-coached athletes. It combines recovery signals, training history, workload, and plan context into clear readiness and workload decisions without writing the athlete's program or becoming a chat coach. Success means the athlete can understand their body, protect long-term progress, and make better training decisions with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, authoritative. The product should feel like a scientific instrument: restrained, legible, and serious without becoming cold or punitive.

## Anti-references

Do not look like a hype-driven fitness scoreboard, a generic wellness dashboard, or a loud orange/red social training app. Avoid chatbot-coach framing, decorative SaaS card grids, warm cream fitness branding, alarmist recovery language, and visual systems where color alone carries meaning.

## Design Principles

Show the decision, not the machinery.
Use restraint as confidence.
Protect athlete autonomy: suggest and explain, never overwrite.
Make physiology understandable without flattening nuance.
Treat trust, privacy, and accessibility as part of the interface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast or better for text and controls. State must never rely on color alone; pair zones with labels and clear copy. Tuwa is intentionally light-only for outdoor, gym-floor, and between-session readability. Support reduced motion, readable numeric displays, and touch targets appropriate for repeated mobile use. Health, recovery, and cycle-related language must avoid diagnosis or shame.
