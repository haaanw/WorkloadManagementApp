## Description (en)

Tuwa is the sports-science layer for self-coached athletes — the people who play their sport, lift, and condition in parallel, and make every training decision themselves.

You author the plan. Tuwa reads your body — HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, and accumulated training load — and tells you exactly how to run today's session: go as written, trim the top set, or hold back. Concrete numbers, not vague scores. Your plan stays yours.

Fully redesigned in 1.6: a calm, precise interface built around one clear daily reading.
