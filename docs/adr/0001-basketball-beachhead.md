# Basketball-anchored beachhead niche

Tuwa's v2.0 wedge launches to a named, findable niche: amateur competitive basketball players who also strength-train (founder = reference user), rather than the generic "self-coached multi-sport/hybrid athlete." A niche you can't find isn't a niche: basketball has real communities, league schedules, and a concrete ASO promise ("stay fresh for game day"), while "multi-sport athlete" is a positioning, not a person. The engine stays multi-modal underneath; the founder's future sports (swimming, climbing, martial arts) become the depth roadmap proving "one fatigue budget, any modality" — sold only after the beachhead holds. We deliberately avoid the market term "hybrid athlete" (endurance+strength, Hyrox/run+lift), which is a different body, community, and fatigue profile.

## Considered Options

- **Basketball-anchored (chosen)** — findable community, one shared fatigue profile, sharp copy; risk: the basketball-and-lifts slice may be thin.
- **Multi-sport-generic** — matches founder's future schedule and the engine's generality, but unfindable and un-nameable at launch.
