# Competitor Card: Apple Watch Recovery Apps

Last updated: 2026-06-06

## Competitors

- Athlytic
- Bevel
- Cora
- Gentler Streak
- Rekov
- FITIV
- PeakWatch
- BodyState
- Sonar

## What This Cluster Sells

Apple Watch already collects enough health data. These apps translate it into recovery, strain, energy, sleep, stress, and training guidance without a second wearable.

## Current Signals

- Athlytic positions around HRV-based recovery, exertion, target exertion, sleep, and Apple Watch widgets. App Store pricing shows $4.99/month and $29.99/year.
- Bevel offers a strong free tier with recovery, sleep, strain, stress, nutrition, strength, energy bank, fitness tracking, health monitors, journal, Apple Watch app, widgets, and complications. Pro is $14.99/month or $99.99/year and unlocks Bevel Intelligence, Health Records, and Biological Age.
- Cora positions as an all-in-one AI health and training platform with recovery, strain, training plans, nutrition, habits, Apple Watch views, templates, and AI coach guidance. App Store in-app purchases show Premium at $9.99 and $39.99.

## Why They Are Dangerous

This cluster is training users to expect daily scores, AI explanations, and Apple Watch-native dashboards. Tuwa cannot win by merely having a recovery score.

Bevel is especially dangerous because it made core tracking free. Cora is especially dangerous because it says "AI coach" clearly and covers training plus nutrition plus recovery in one story.

## Their Weaknesses

- Broad health scope can dilute serious training specificity.
- Strength workflows are improving but are still not always built around serious RPE/RIR, templates, PRs, fatigue, and program adaptation.
- AI advice can feel generic if it cannot show the exact training logic.
- Privacy and data locality are usually less central than in Tuwa.

## Tuwa Counterposition

Tuwa should be the serious training product inside the Apple Watch recovery market:

> Not a health dashboard. Not another score. A training adjustment system for strength and hybrid athletes.

## Messaging To Test

- "For lifters who need more than a recovery score."
- "The Apple Watch training coach built around your actual sets, reps, RPE, sleep, and HRV."
- "Know whether to push, cut volume, swap session type, or recover."
- "Recovery apps show the signal. Tuwa changes the session."

## Product Implications

- Make workout logging faster than Strong/Hevy for repeated training days.
- Make the daily recommendation visibly tied to today's planned session.
- Show exact adjustment logic: cut volume by X%, cap RPE at Y, choose session type Z.
- Build a weekly review that makes the athlete feel coached, not just scored.
- Consider comparison pages: Athlytic alternative, Bevel alternative for strength training, Cora alternative for lifters.
