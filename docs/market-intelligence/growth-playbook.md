# Growth Playbook

Last updated: 2026-06-06

## Growth Thesis

Tuwa should grow by owning a narrow problem that current apps do not explain well:

> I lift and condition. My Apple Watch, recovery app, and workout log all show different things. What should I change in today's training?

The product should become the answer to that question.

## 90-Day Plan

### Days 1-14: Fix Positioning And Launch Surface

Goals:

- Tuwa name everywhere in public surfaces.
- One primary promise.
- App Store metadata and screenshots aligned around session adjustment.

Actions:

- Align Tuwa copy across app, metadata, legal pages, PDF export, screenshots, website, support pages, and comparison pages.
- Update App Store subtitle to stay concrete. Good options:
  - "Adaptive Strength Training"
  - "Recovery-Guided Strength"
  - "Train Smarter With HRV"
- Rework first screenshot sequence:
  1. "Know what to do today"
  2. "Adjust volume from recovery and load"
  3. "Log strength fast"
  4. "Review what worked this week"
  5. "Share progress with your coach"
- Remove ACWR from top-level marketing unless explaining advanced workload.

### Days 15-30: Build The First Acquisition Assets

Goals:

- Capture high-intent search from users comparing tools.
- Give communities something useful, not promotional.

Assets:

- Free tool: "Strength Training Readiness Calculator"
  - Inputs: HRV vs baseline, RHR vs baseline, sleep, soreness, recent training days, planned session intensity.
  - Output: push, maintain, reduce, swap, recover.
  - CTA: "Want this from Apple Health automatically? Try Tuwa."
- Free article/tool: "Apple Watch Training Load For Lifters: What It Misses"
- Free article/tool: "How To Adjust Strength Volume When HRV Is Low"
- Comparison pages:
  - Athlytic alternative for strength training
  - Bevel alternative for lifters
  - Cora vs Tuwa
  - WHOOP Strength Trainer alternative with Apple Watch
  - Strong vs Tuwa
  - Hevy vs Tuwa

### Days 31-60: Community And Beta Loop

Goals:

- Get real user language.
- Find the strongest wedge audience.
- Make the product better from training diaries, not guesses.

Target communities:

- Apple Watch fitness users
- Strength training app users
- HYROX and hybrid training groups
- CrossFit and functional fitness athletes
- Natural bodybuilding and powerbuilding communities
- Tactical athletes and recreational team-sport athletes
- Independent strength coaches

Approach:

- Lead with educational posts: "How I use HRV without letting it ruin training."
- Share free calculators, not direct app pitches.
- Ask for training diary examples and show how the adjustment logic would work.
- Offer 20 beta slots for serious lifters/hybrid athletes.
- Offer 5 coach beta slots for small rosters.

Interview questions:

- What do you use to log workouts?
- What recovery app or wearable do you trust?
- When recovery is low, what do you actually change?
- What would make you ignore a readiness score?
- What was the last time you overreached?
- Would you pay for a weekly review that told you what to adjust next week?

### Days 61-90: Retention And Referral Hooks

Goals:

- Make Tuwa useful after novelty fades.
- Create shareable outputs.

Build or emphasize:

- Weekly Training Review:
  - Readiness trend
  - Workload trend
  - PRs
  - Missed/modified sessions
  - Soreness and fatigue patterns
  - Next-week recommendation
- Shareable export:
  - Coach PDF
  - Screenshot card
  - "This week I adjusted volume by X% because..."
- Referral loop:
  - "Share with your coach"
  - "Invite training partner"
  - "Compare your weekly training review"

## Channel Strategy

Website:

- Home page focused on "adaptive strength training."
- Comparison pages.
- Free readiness calculator.
- Educational articles.
- Privacy page that explains HealthKit boundaries in plain language.

Email:

- Waitlist/beta onboarding.
- Weekly training review samples.
- "One useful adjustment per week" newsletter for serious amateurs.

In-app:

- Sunday review prompt.
- "What changed this week?" card.
- Upgrade prompts around history, weekly review, coach export, and plan adjustment.

### Rented Channels

- Apple Watch fitness
- Strength training
- Natural bodybuilding
- HYROX

TikTok/Instagram:

YouTube:

- "Apple Watch training load explained for lifters."

Offer:

## Content Pillars

1. Recovery score is not the decision.
2. Apple Watch training load is useful but incomplete for lifters.
3. How to adjust volume and intensity without losing progress.
4. HRV for strength athletes without superstition.

## Paid Acquisition

Do not start broad paid ads until the landing page and onboarding convert organically.

First paid tests:

- Apple Search Ads on exact competitor/intent terms:
  - recovery app Apple Watch
  - HRV training app
  - strength training Apple Watch
  - workout readiness
  - training load Apple Watch
- Retarget visitors to free calculator.
- Small creator whitelisting if organic posts validate messaging.

## Metrics

Activation:

- HealthKit connected.
- First workout logged or imported.
- First recommendation viewed.
- First planned session adjusted.

Retention:

- 3 logged sessions in first 10 days.
- Weekly review viewed.
- User returns after first low-readiness day.
- User edits workout based on recommendation.

Monetization:

- Upgrade after viewing history limit.
- Upgrade after weekly review preview.
- Upgrade after coach export/share.
- Annual conversion rate by segment.

Referral:

- Share weekly review.
- Invite coach.
- Invite training partner.

## First Growth Bet

The highest-probability bet is:

> Launch a free "Apple Watch Strength Readiness Calculator" plus comparison pages, then convert users into a beta where Tuwa automatically generates the same guidance from HealthKit and their workout log.

This lets Tuwa test demand before depending on App Store discovery.
