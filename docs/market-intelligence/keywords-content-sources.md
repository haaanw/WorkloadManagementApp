## Content Formats To Produce

### Comparison Pages

Best first pages:

1. `Athlytic Alternative For Strength Training`
2. `Bevel Alternative For Lifters`
3. `Cora vs Tuwa`
4. `WHOOP Strength Trainer Alternative For Apple Watch`
5. `Strong vs Tuwa`
6. `Hevy vs Tuwa`
7. `Apple Watch Training Load For Lifters`

Structure:

- Who the competitor is best for.
- Where the competitor wins.
- Where it falls short for strength/hybrid training.
- How Tuwa is different.
- Honest "do not use Tuwa if..." section.
