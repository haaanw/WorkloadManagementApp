## Current Strategic Vote

Tuwa should not position as another generic recovery score, Apple Watch health dashboard, or broad AI health coach. Those lanes are now crowded by Apple, Google/Fitbit, Bevel, Cora, Athlytic, WHOOP, Garmin, and Oura.

The best wedge is:

> An explainable self-coaching system for serious strength and hybrid athletes who already use Apple Watch and want their training plan, workout log, recovery, load, and weekly training review in one adaptive loop.

The strongest go-to-market angle is not "we have ACWR." It is:

> Stop guessing whether to push, cut volume, or recover. Tuwa turns your actual lifting, workload, HRV, sleep, and soreness into today's training adjustment.
